Meet NONON

NONON is a helper for the files you already have. Say what you need in your own words. It does the work on your computer, shows you the result, and asks before it changes anything.

What it does

Three things NONON does.

Give it your files and say what you need. Here is what it can do today.

Your control

You stay in charge. NONON asks first.

NONON is a helper, not the boss. It asks before it changes anything.

  1. 01

    It shows you first.

    NONON shows what it wants to change and waits for your OK.

  2. 02

    It keeps a backup.

    Before it changes a file, it saves a copy of the original.

  3. 03

    You can undo.

    Changed your mind? Undo it. If you edited the file since, NONON stops and tells you. It will not erase your work.

How it works.

Three steps. You do not need to learn how to write prompts.

Questions

Questions people ask.

Is it free?

NONON is free to use. Online AI services you connect may charge you on their own plans.

Is it open source?

Yes. NONON is open source under the Apache License 2.0, and the code is on GitHub. You can read it, build it or fork it. Source code on GitHub (opens in a new tab).

Does it need the internet?

Only once, to download the AI (3 to 6 GB). After that, its core jobs work with no connection. Gmail and online AI always need the internet, because those services do.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Not by default. Your files and the AI stay on your computer, and this site has no tracking. Setup downloads the AI from public websites. If you turn on Claude, Codex, Antigravity or Gmail, the content of that job goes to that service. NONON tells you before it does. Read the privacy page for the full list.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), and Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later). It does not run on Intel Macs, Linux or phones. Aim for 8 GB of memory at least. 16 GB is better.

Which AI does it use?

By default, a small open AI that runs on your computer. You can also connect Claude, Codex or Antigravity if you already use them. They are optional.

What if the AI is wrong?

A small AI can make mistakes. So NONON adds up the numbers with ordinary code, shows where each result came from, and marks what is missing. It never changes your files until you say OK. Check its work the way you would check a new helper's.

Can I undo a change?

Yes. NONON saves a copy of the original before it changes a file, and you can undo from there. If you edited the file after that, NONON stops and tells you instead of writing over your work.

Who made it?
