# brand-src

Copies of the official files from `brand/NONON-brand-pack-v1/` that the site builds from, so `site/` builds without the pack.
Do not edit by hand; replace from the pack.

- `logos/`, `icons/`, `app-icons/`, `motion/non-idle.svg`, `fonts/` (Nunito variable TTF and OFL.txt): unchanged from the pack.
- `mascots/non-*-fit.png`: the fitted 3D poses from `brand/derived/` (one 325x461 frame, character centred, feet on y=392, soft
  shadow fading to nothing before every edge). These replace the pack's own PNGs, which sit in the lower right of a padded square
  with faint shadow pixels out to the border. Non is the Pragma mascot Pip, used with permission of its owner.
