Status

What works today

We would rather tell you plainly than let a menu item suggest something works when it does not. This is an early release.

Last reviewed 9 October 2026.

NONON is open source under the Apache License 2.0. The code is on GitHub: github.com/systoai-design/appbuildersph-nonon.

The basics

  • The AI on your computerA small AI runs on your computer and reads and writes your files. While it answered, it only talked to NONON on the same computer.
    Works
  • Working offline after setupSetup downloads the AI once, 3 to 6 GB. After that, the core jobs below do not need the internet.
    Works
  • First-run setupChecks your computer, picks an AI that fits, downloads it, pauses and resumes, and checks that it downloaded correctly.
    Works
  • Results shown inside the appSpreadsheets and documents open in the app. You can also open them in their own program.
    Works
  • Changes wait for your OK, with backup and undoNONON shows a change first, saves a backup, and can undo it. If you edited the file afterward, it stops and tells you instead of writing over your work.
    Works

The jobs

  • Compare two spreadsheetsExcel (.xlsx) and .csv files. It finds matches, rows in only one file, rows listed twice, and rows it is not sure about. Code adds up the money. Macros, links to other workbooks and fancy formatting are not supported yet.
    Works, with limits
  • Turn meeting notes into a follow-upText, Word and PDF files that hold real text. Scanned PDFs are not read. The AI is small, so check the names and dates it lists.
    Works, with limits
  • Turn a reading into a study sheetSame file types as above. You get explanations, practice questions and an answer key, based on your reading. Check the answers before you hand them out.
    Works, with limits
  • Plan a lessonA first-draft outline from your material, with its assumptions stated. Same file types as above.
    Works, with limits
  • Draft a documentA short summary and a draft reply or report from one file, with the lines each part came from.
    Works, with limits
  • Tidy a folderLists every move first. Nothing moves until you approve. It never writes over a file or moves one out of your project folder.
    Works
  • Run a job on a scheduleSet it up in plain words, like every weekday at 8. It runs while your computer is on and awake. We tested it with a real AI and a pretend clock, not by waiting for days.
    Works, with limits
  • Draft a document with several AIs in turnOne AI makes a plan, one writes, one checks. We ran it fully on this computer, and once with Codex for the writing step. The checking AI can be wrong, so the notes say so.
    Works, with limits

Optional connections

Off until you turn them on. When you use them, the content of that job leaves your computer. You pay for online AI on your own plan.

  • CodexA short real test job ran and could be cancelled. Longer jobs are not tested.
    Works, with limits
  • AntigravityA short real test job ran and could be cancelled. Longer jobs are not tested.
    Works, with limits
  • ClaudeClaude was not signed in on our test computer, so no real job ran. We checked that NONON shows a clear message to sign in.
    Built, not fully tested yet
  • GmailReads only the messages you allow and writes a brief with the AI on your computer. It cannot send or delete mail. We tested it against a pretend Google, not a real Google account yet.
    Built, not fully tested yet
  • Sharing AI between your own computers on one networkYou pair two of your computers with a code, and one lends its AI to the other. Built and tested on one computer. Not tried on two yet.
    Built, not fully tested yet

Computers

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)The installer is built and the app runs on our Windows 11 test computer. The download page says whether this version is code-signed.
    Works
  • Mac with Apple siliconThe app opens on an Apple silicon Mac. We have tested it much less than on Windows.
    Built, not fully tested yet
  • Computers with 8 GB or 16 GB of memoryWe aim for 8 GB at least and 16 GB is better. Our test computer has far more, so we have not tested these sizes yet.
    Built, not fully tested yet

Not yet: a phone app, Linux, Intel Macs, scanned PDFs, and other characters. For now, Non is your helper.
