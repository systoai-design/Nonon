## Key ideas

**How Plants Make Their Food**: Plants use a process called photosynthesis to turn light energy into chemical energy stored in sugar. This happens inside tiny green structures called chloroplasts in the leaves.

> **photosynthesis.txt:3** *"Photosynthesis is the process plants use to turn light energy into chemical energy stored in sugar."*

**What Goes In and What Comes Out**: Plants need light, water, and carbon dioxide to make glucose and oxygen. The roots take up water, and carbon dioxide enters through small openings called stomata.

> **photosynthesis.txt:9** *"A plant needs three things for photosynthesis: light, water and carbon dioxide."*

**The Two Stages**: Photosynthesis happens in two stages: the light reactions and the Calvin cycle. The light reactions split water to release oxygen and make energy molecules, while the Calvin cycle turns carbon dioxide into sugar.

> **photosynthesis.txt:14** *"Photosynthesis happens in two connected stages."*

**Factors affecting speed**: Three main things change how fast photosynthesis runs: light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration, and temperature. Very high temperatures slow the process because enzymes stop working properly above about 40 degrees Celsius.

> **photosynthesis.txt:22** *"[22] Three main factors change how fast photosynthesis runs: light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration and temperature."*
