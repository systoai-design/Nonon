# Harbor Lane Bakery: online ordering brief

## Current Operations and Needs

The shop sells about 120 loaves and 60 pastries on a normal weekday, and phone orders are missed on busy mornings, with Maria estimating 8 to 10 calls a week go unanswered. Most regular customers live within 2 km of the shop and pick up their order, as delivery is not wanted for now. The shop opens at 6:30 am and closes at 3:00 pm, Tuesday to Sunday. Maria wants a page that lists the daily bread and pastries with prices, allows customers to choose a pickup time and pay in the shop rather than online, and sends an email to the shop for each order so nothing is missed.

## Project Constraints

The budget for the first version is 1,500 USD in total, and the site must go live before the Saturday market on 14 November. Maria does not want customer accounts or a loyalty scheme in the first version. Everything must be easy for staff who are not technical to update, as prices and the daily list change often.

## Open Questions

It is undecided who will update the daily list each morning, as Maria thinks it could be the opening baker. Whether to offer a cut-off time for same-day orders has not been decided.
