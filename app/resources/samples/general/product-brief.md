# Harbor Lane Bakery: online ordering brief

Harbor Lane Bakery is a neighbourhood bakery with one shop and four staff. Today every order is taken by phone or at the counter, and the owner, Maria Santos, wants customers to be able to order ahead on a simple website.

## What we know

- The shop sells about 120 loaves and 60 pastries on a normal weekday.
- Phone orders are missed on busy mornings; Maria estimates 8 to 10 calls a week go unanswered.
- Most regular customers live within 2 km of the shop and pick up their order. Delivery is not wanted for now.
- The shop opens at 6:30 am and closes at 3:00 pm, Tuesday to Sunday.

## What Maria wants

- A page that lists the daily bread and pastries with prices.
- Customers choose a pickup time and pay in the shop, not online.
- An email to the shop for each order, so nothing is missed.

## Limits

- Budget for the first version is 1,500 USD in total.
- The site must go live before the Saturday market on 14 November.
- Maria does not want customer accounts or a loyalty scheme in the first version.
- Everything must be easy for staff who are not technical to update: prices and the daily list change often.

## Open questions

- Who will update the daily list each morning? Maria thinks it could be the opening baker.
- Whether to offer a cut-off time for same-day orders has not been decided.
